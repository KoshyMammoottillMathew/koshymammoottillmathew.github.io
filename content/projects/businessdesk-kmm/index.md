## BusinessDesk by KMM

**BusinessDesk by KMM** is a privacy-first, local-first business management workspace designed to bring day-to-day business operations into one application.

It combines finance, customer management, sales, invoicing, quotations, projects, inventory, team management, goals and reporting in a single dashboard while keeping business data in the user's browser.

### Core Modules

- Dashboard with business overview and operational insights
- Finance and transaction management
- Customer relationship management and Customer 360 views
- Sales and opportunity tracking
- Invoice creation and payment workflows
- Quotations with conversion to invoices
- Project management and progress tracking
- Inventory, stock movements and low-stock visibility
- Team management
- Goals and progress tracking
- Business reports
- Settings, backup and restore

### Connected Business Workflows

BusinessDesk is designed so records can work together rather than operating as isolated modules. Quotations can be converted into invoices, paid invoices can create linked finance transactions, inventory products and services can be selected during quotation and invoice creation, and customer records provide a consolidated view of related business activity.

Invoice payment records are linked with Finance to reduce duplicate entry. Stock movements are handled through controlled Stock In, Stock Out and adjustment workflows rather than silently changing inventory quantities when an invoice is created.

### Privacy-first, local-first architecture

BusinessDesk stores application data locally in the browser using IndexedDB. The current architecture does not require a remote business database or account system for normal use.

Because the data is local to the browser and device, BusinessDesk includes backup and restore functionality so users can maintain their own copy of business data.
